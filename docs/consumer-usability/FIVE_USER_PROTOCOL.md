# Uncoached comprehension test — owner to run

**Prepared, not conducted.** No participants have been recruited or tested by the agent. Browser emulation is not evidence of human comprehension or physical-iPhone usability. This is a small qualitative release gate, not a statistically representative study or an investment test.

Recruit five consenting first-time visitors who have not seen the product or the development discussion. Include people unfamiliar with token/quote terminology; record familiarity as none/some, not identifying information. Prefer their own iPhone/Safari where available; record the actual model, iOS, browser and text size, including desktop participants if used. Do not ask for wallet addresses, balances, accounts, personal tokens, purchases or funds. Do not record names, video or audio without separate consent. Keep anonymous P1–P5 notes locally; do not commit personal data.

Use the reviewed, owner-approved homepage build. Record URL, deployed Git SHA, date/time and network status. Do not use an unreviewed preview as proof of live production. Confirm the exact-mint links and financial withholding before inviting anyone. If publication rights have not been approved for the displayed metadata, stop and resolve that owner gate before public testing. No new source refresh or permissions are implied by this protocol.

## Facilitator words and timing

Read only: “We are testing the page, not you. Please use it as you normally would. You can stop at any time. There is no purchase task.” Do not explain the product, token ownership, quote assets, evidence sections or where to tap. Do not mention the answers below. Do not correct answers until the session ends. If asked for help, say “Please do what you would normally do”; log the request as assistance. Stop a task at 30 seconds without coaching and record incomplete.

1. Show `/` for exactly five seconds, then cover the screen. Ask: **“What do you think this page helps you do?”** Record the answer verbatim before showing it again. Score purpose correct only if it describes understanding/explaining a token and checking evidence/unknowns, rather than buying, earning, universal search or current pricing.
2. Reset to `/`. Say: **“Choose an example and find out what it is.”** Start the 30-second completion timer now; stop when the participant says they have an answer. Record first action, selected token, time, wrong turns, scrolls and any assistance. Alternate starting examples P1/P4 Super Inu, P2/P5 RuneScape Gold, P3 Grokification only if the participant asks which to choose; log that prompt separately. Do not point to a button or force an example.
3. Ask, without supplying answer choices: **“If someone bought this token, what would they hold? What does its relationship with the named paired asset mean? What does this page establish about payments to that person?”** Record verbatim, then score using the private rubric. Do not ask them to buy anything.
4. Say: **“Find something important that this page does not know. Tell me whether the evidence describes today.”** Allow up to 30 seconds. Record what they found, time, and whether they understood the original capture date without help. Then ask **“What would you still need to know before relying on this?”**
5. Say: **“Find where the explanation came from and when it was captured.”** Time up to 30 seconds. Success means identifying a relevant source receipt and its original capture date; navigating away is optional. If opening a provider endpoint, its current response does not refresh this captured explanation. Log whether the participant wrongly treats it as a payment proof, rights permission or live update.
6. Say: **“Send this exact example to yourself without an account, then return and start another search.”** Do not require sending a real message: copying the exact-mint URL and demonstrating where it goes is sufficient. Time up to 30 seconds; record native clipboard/fallback, search/focus confusion, and back navigation. Never enter private input.

Ask last: “Which words or actions made you hesitate?” Only after recording all answers, explain any misconceptions and restate that there are no verified holder payments, returns, underlying-stock ownership or fresh financial figures. End without investment guidance.

## Private scoring rubric

| Measure | Correct interpretation | Failure examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual holding | The selected Solana base token, e.g. Super Inu, identified by its exact address | Holding NVIDIA equity/NVDAX automatically, gold/GLDX or SpaceX equity/SPCXX automatically |
| Pair meaning | A recorded trading relationship with a different quote token; provider labels are not ownership, backing or redemption proof | “NVDAX means I own NVIDIA”; quote name/ticker alone identifies the asset |
| Verified rewards | Dated provider-reported configuration only | Guaranteed eligibility, payments, profit, APY or current reward flow |
| Unknown/risk | Notices stale evidence, unknown payouts/eligibility, incomplete examples or withheld figures/rights; understands at least one consequence | “Verified means safe”; no risks noticed even after the prompt |
| Time | Captured 14 Oct 2026; stale, not current; provider-generated time is separate | Today’s settings/prices, or opening an endpoint updates the receipt |
| Verification | Finds the relevant source and original capture date unaided | Badge alone, hash as provider signature/license, endpoint as holder payment proof |
| Sharing/search | Exact `/?mint=` example, followed by another search; no login/wallet | Shares a ticker as identity, loses the result or cannot find search |

## Go / no-go, decided before testing

Human approval remains required regardless of score. Continue only if at least **4/5** correctly identify the purpose at five seconds, **4/5** complete example → explanation unaided within 30 seconds, **4/5** find a risk and original source/date unaided within each task’s 30 seconds, and **4/5** share then locate another search without help. Require **5/5** correct after reading about what they actually hold, absence of underlying stock/gold ownership, stale evidence and no verified holder payouts/returns. Any material misconception, hidden warning, accessibility blocker, financial disclosure or publication-rights issue is a **no-go**: record the failure, fix the narrow cause, and test again with fresh visitors. Do not average away harmful misunderstanding or label assisted completion a pass.

Publish only anonymous aggregate counts with the actual denominator, device mix, exact build and limitations after tests occur. Leave this document marked **not conducted** until an owner appends dated evidence; do not fill the template with invented participants or timings.
