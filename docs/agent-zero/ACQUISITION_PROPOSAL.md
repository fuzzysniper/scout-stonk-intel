## Recommendation and the decision advantage to test

Authorize **C0 source qualification of a private GeckoTerminal Solana new-pool + completed one-minute OHLCV adapter**, conditional on current documentation and written use/retention permission. C0 is at most **six data GET attempts, one mechanically selected exact pool, $0 purchased services**, and no automatic retry. It is not authorization for ongoing collection, live capital or C1 below. If source clocks, units, access, rights or zero-cost entitlement cannot be established, **do not acquire data**. No fallback provider is implicitly approved.

This is the smallest promising addition because one source could supply both a bounded discovery feed and interval observations for the same exact pool. StonkFun remains reusable venue/quote metadata; its market-cap-sorted catalog cannot establish an early launch feed or timed performance. The existing mint-facts work answers control questions, not what is emerging. Agent B's production proof, one-shot budgets and source gates remain entirely independent.

The first measurable advantage would be **earlier, reproducible awareness of activity in a defined sampled pool universe**, quantified by observation delay, available qualification lead time and later observed expansion in that same pool. It would not establish predicted 5x–20x, executable gains, all-Solana discovery or alpha. A provider may index a pool after its initial expansion; we must be able to reject the premise using those results.

## 2. Minimum evidence contract, before a qualification can mean anything

Every field carries separate assurance (`provider_reported` / `finalized_rpc_reported` / `unavailable`) and quality (`available` / `not_observed` / `conflicting` / `stale` / `unsupported` / `missing_interval`), plus evidence reference and actual availability time. Source assurance must not disappear when an observation becomes stale. These are conceptual research states, not changes to shared backend contracts. Unknown is not zero, absent, safe or disqualified. Keep hypotheses separate from facts.

| Dimension | Minimum fields / semantics | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery and scope | Network; exact base mint, quote mint, pool address, venue/program claim; source endpoint/version; first received UTC; provider-indexed time if documented; page/cursor/count/truncation; complete=false unless proven. | Pool creation is not mint creation. First SCOUT receipt is not first trade or “before expansion.” Deduplicate pools by network/pool and candidates by exact base mint; preserve all other pool identities. Never ticker-match or substitute a newly liquid pool retrospectively. |
| Token/pool age | Provider-reported pool creation time separately from finalized initialization signature, slot, nullable blockTime and actual mint-init evidence. | Current mint-facts context slot is the **check slot**, not a birth slot. A “new pool” may contain an old/relaunched token. Missing history or null blockTime means age unknown. |
| Price and persistence | Original decimal strings; explicit price numerator/denominator mints or USD methodology; completed interval `[start,end)`; OHLC, nonoverlapping volume and documented units; no-trade/fill/revision semantics; retrieval and first-availability UTC. | A completed bar is an interval, not an exact last-trade timestamp. Require documented close/volume semantics and positive actual interval volume; no synthetic empty bars. Preserve zero separately from missing. No rolling-24h subtraction, summed rolling windows or HTTP-Date-as-market-time. |
| Liquidity | Exact pool/quote, source unit and actual observation interval/time, reserves where supported, price/tick context, source precision and pool migration. | USD TVL/reserve repricing is not net inflow or executable depth. Untimed `reserve_in_usd` remains snapshot context and cannot establish a liquidity change. |
| Activity | Disjoint interval volume, base/quote orientation, buy/sell counts with interval ends, optional deduplicated transaction signatures and completeness. | Counts are not unique people, wallet demand or genuine activity. Wash/self-trading can mimic acceleration. An OHLCV-only source leaves buy/sell and participant quality NOT OBSERVED. |
| Supply / controls | A genuinely acquired, retained, unexpired MintFactsV1: exact raw supply/decimals, original program, authority states, supported extension interpretation, finalized slot, original checked UTC and RPC response hash. | Preserve `production_rpc`/recorded/synthetic distinctions and Token-2022 unsupported extensions. No automatic call, budget reuse, gate flip or dependence on Agent B deploying. Transfer fees/hooks/permanent delegates and related controls require explicit reviewed interpretation. |
| Concentration / relationships | Attributable raw account balances at comparable finalized slots; denominator/exclusions; known pools/vaults; owner aggregation and uncertainty; provenance of any deployer/funding links. | Top token accounts are not top beneficial owners. `getTokenLargestAccounts` alone is incomplete, and holder count alone gives no concentration. Common funding is not proof of common control. Deferred indexing/privacy/rights approval; no wallet traversal now. |
| Execution / exit | Verified pool program/state, direction, input size/base units, timestamped quote/route context, output minimum, fees/transfer costs, price impact, native SOL fees and quote conversion, independently supported exit capacity. | Indicative price/TVL is not a fill or guaranteed sale. CLMM ticks, CPMM reserves and routing need different models. A burned LP token is not universal proof of liquidity control. Read-only quote/simulation work requires separate authorization and still cannot guarantee a future exit. |

For future bar qualification, retain bar boundaries and clock uncertainty explicitly. The latest **completed, traded** one-minute bar must arrive within a proposed 90 seconds of its end; request/response clocks must be consistent and available before the decision. This is a new, unvalidated research-bar policy, not relaxation of existing snapshot, tick or reward gates. A documented close may lie anywhere within its minute; no exact trade time is invented. Older intervals retained later remain late-available history. If the provider fills bars, has unknown close/volume units, or cannot distinguish traded intervals, withhold the affected calculation.

Use bounded exact-decimal/rational arithmetic, never floating-point price ratios or rescaled ticker units. Quarantine conflicting duplicate bars; retain revisions as new versions with their later availability time. A price tick, completed candle, live snapshot and execution quote remain distinct types. Pool migration ends one series; it cannot splice into another without a separately reviewed continuity rule.

## 5. C1: first validation experiment, a separate approval

Only after C0 and a reviewed bar adapter: an owner-operated, finite private capture, **not a public/scheduled collector or production deployment**. No authentic RPC, Helius, wallet or quote requests are part of C1. The experiment asks whether useful pre-expansion observations can be retained, not whether to spend the $10 sleeve. Live speculative allocation remains $0.

- Discovery: 24 one-page polls (≤20 records/page), every five minutes for two hours (ticks 0…23). Register every returned identity and rejection reason before seeing outcomes. Enroll the **first six distinct exact base-mint/pool subjects** mechanically, without price/risk selection; pin each initial wSOL pool. Preserve repeats, other pools and capacity-excluded candidates. Report source ordering, truncation and capacity censoring; this is not all eligible Solana tokens.
- Follow every enrolled subject—including rejected/unknown-risk cases—for 25 hours from admission. At most 300 OHLCV reads/subject, each covering at most five completed one-minute bars; no historical-page traversal, gap repair or replacement winners. Six one-time detail reads at most. Maximum elapsed study 27 hours. The extra follow-up hour covers a fixed first checkpoint around admission +30 minutes, anchoring/entry-reference delay and its full 24-hour outcome horizon; late checkpoints remain censored.
- **Request ceiling:** `24 discovery + 6 detail + (6 × 300) bars = 1,830 data attempts`. C0's six are separate: combined maximum 1,836, never an automatic rollover. Peaks ≤6 attempts per rolling minute, serial ≥10 seconds apart; average ≤1.22/min over 25 hours even if the full cap were spent then. Actual provider entitlement may be lower and always wins. No wallet/benchmark/quote calls hidden in this budget.
- Original response bytes, normalized evidence and metadata only with rights; private owner storage outside Git, ≤128 MiB cumulative. Source/body caps and request counts include failures; **the first cap reached stops acquisition**. At maximum allowed response sizes storage can be exhausted before all 1,830 calls; do not claim the full study is guaranteed. No purchased service/hosting/subscription; existing-PC bandwidth, electricity and operator time are unpriced, not free. Any required new spend/key/account is a separate owner decision.
- Data-quality target, proposed before execution: ≥95% expected discovery polls, no observed discovery-poll gap >10 minutes, zero identity/unit conflicts accepted, all decisions anchored before eligible outcome bars, and ≥95% expected completed bars per enrolled subject. A specific scored 20-minute feature or 24-hour outcome requires **every required interval**, with no imputation; aggregate 95% does not excuse a missing scoring boundary or interior bar. These targets are not reliability results. The source samples can test awareness before a **subsequent observed expansion**; they cannot establish that this was before the token's first expansion, before other pools, or before other traders noticed it.

Log measured discovery delay relative to provider-reported pool creation (when available), first-seen bar age, source request latency, revision/duplicate rate and missing intervals. Do not name this true launch-to-detection latency without independently established creation history. Persist a missing interval and its cause instead of an invented flat price. A venue outage, vanished pool, capacity exclusion and no actual trading remain distinct.

### What would be required to prove economic edge later

C1 measures provider-reported price paths in exact wSOL quote units, **not holder ROI, USD returns or executable treasury P&L**. No native SOL/USD benchmark, pool-depth/sellability proof, fees, concentration or finalized token controls are acquired. Token performance relative to its quote does not establish outperformance of a USD/SOL portfolio. No multiple is a predicted outcome.

A later, separately budgeted forward cohort must freeze: (a) actual contemporaneous size-aware entry/exit assumptions and costs, (b) an independently timed SOL benchmark in the same numéraire, (c) an all-eligible equal-notional baseline without survivor replacement, and (d) a simple first-qualifying-quote/fixed-horizon rule with identical eligibility, clocks and cost gates. Record maximum adverse/favorable excursion and chronological drawdown only over supported observations; sampled extrema are not true extrema. Model fees/slippage in original units with conservative rounding and measured conversion evidence; count failed exits and censoring, never imply fills from bars. Keep gross price changes separate from net outcomes and capital at risk.

Require a preregistered sample-size/power plan, uncertainty for strategy-minus-baseline results, dependence/venue regime and multiple-testing treatment. A 30-day process audit, six-token pilot or an isolated 20× cannot establish alpha. If source data cannot support the baseline or a cost-aware loss, report that exact failure and stop the economic claim rather than enlarging permissions or cherry-picking a new cohort.
