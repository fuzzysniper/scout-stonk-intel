# UNFARMD / exportable art briefs

Working prompts for founder/artist use after direction selection. **Not commissioned final art, production-asset approval, a license guarantee or trademark clearance.** No Midjourney request was made in this task. Current local SVGs are original code-authored layout studies. The owner supplies final chosen assets separately; record the artist/tool terms and permitted commercial use before publication.

Do not ask the generator to produce the wordmark, UI text, charts or token statistics. Those belong in accessible HTML. Review every output for accidental likeness to existing meme IP and remove any logos. Parameters below are editable composition suggestions, not assertions about a specific model version’s behavior.

## Rights and publication checklist

1. Founder chooses one direction and whether there is a hero mascot at all.
2. Keep prompt, tool/model/version, generation date, selected output and artist revisions; no private portfolio positions or watch history in references.
3. Verify current tool subscription/commercial terms and the artist agreement. Resolve provenance and recognizable-IP issues; do not treat a prompt as permission or exclusivity.
4. Commission final scalable artwork/mark, accessible contrast variants and mobile recomposition. Review humor, accidental symbols and source-status implications.
5. Perform separate trademark/domain naming review. No clearance, registration or domain purchase has been done.
6. Only a separately approved implementation may replace the public SCOUT identity. Art selection does not authorize source acquisition, signals, provider activation or production deployment.
