## Product decision and proposed copy

**SCOUT — The Pair Intelligence Terminal**  
**Follow the pair. Verify the flow.**

SCOUT explains an exact pair's economic mechanisms, recipients and evidence for observed flows. Market discovery helps readers find a subject; it does not decide which asset is an investment opportunity. StonkFun remains the first supported integration. Venue-agnostic architecture is a design direction, not a statement that other venues are integrated.

| Surface, after a separately approved copy change | Proposed text / constraint |

| First-screen purpose | “Understand a pair's fees, recipients and recorded distributions.” |
| Visible support disclosure | “StonkFun is currently the supported reward source.” |
| Scope on a research result | “Solana · StonkFun · bounded sample” plus actual pool-link confidence; separate market provider/pool when different |

| Interval finding | “Observed counter change” with actual period/unit and coverage; “Validated delivered payouts” requires a separately proven chain sequence |
| Historical research | “Historical, not current” and original capture date; financial figures withheld while rights are unverified |
| Holder outcome | “Holder returns not established”; no ROI/APY, projected yield or “top earners” |
| Sample ordering | Name by default in the proposed economics view. Liquidity/volume remain explicitly scoped context sorts, never reward/economic rankings |
| Future venue disclosure | In methodology/roadmap: “Pump.fun, Raydium and Meteora are research candidates, not supported reward integrations.” No enabled venue tabs or coverage counters |

Primary reader task: **identify an exact pair → understand how value could move and to whom → inspect what was actually observed and withheld**. Quote-asset exploration and token search remain quick entry points. Do not make readers first study a schema or finish a market leaderboard. Use one concise economic conclusion, then Evidence/Details; show critical source, coverage, identity and rights warnings before disclosure.

Keep SCOUT's own name, methodology and navigation identity. STORK community/treasury is separately branded. Proposed relationship copy: “SCOUT is independent research with an informational STORK ecosystem connection. That relationship does not influence inclusion, sorting or evidence gates.” Any actual operator holdings, sponsorship or affiliated asset inclusion must receive an applicable conflict disclosure before publication; do not assert that undisclosed holdings do or do not exist. No STORK entitlement, preferred ranking, token gate or treasury benefit is proposed.

## Economic vocabulary: no interchangeable “rewards” field

| Concept | Meaning | Never infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trading fees | Trade/bonding-curve charge at a specified market, asset and fee schedule; may be split among recipients | Fee revenue from volume × a guessed flat rate; all fees belonging to token holders |
| Creator fees | Creator-directed share/accrual/claim, if that exact configuration is evidenced | Holder distributions, creator cash received from an unclaimed balance, or incremental revenue on top of the same trading fee |
| Holder distributions | Gross reported distributions or validated deliveries to a defined eligible holder population | Equal per-holder payment, holder profit, creator fees, or pending balances already delivered |
| Incentive emissions | Program-funded token incentives with defined asset, recipient and vesting/claim rules | Trading-fee yield or cash income at the token's current price |
| Buybacks | Verified purchase/execution with inventory/burn disposition separately evidenced | Token distribution to holders, a price floor, profitable holders or guaranteed price support |
| Distribution history | Dated cumulative reports, observed counter deltas or validated payout events, each labeled by basis | A synchronized payout sequence from two snapshots; lifetime amounts as recent flow |
| Liquidity / volume | Point-in-time reported liquidity inventory and separately defined reported turnover interval | Revenue, executable depth/slippage, locked capital, historical trend or realized return |
| Price performance | Exact-asset/pool/orientation change over actual compatible market observations | Total return, receipt of any distributions, or equity/metal ownership from a ticker |
| Realized holder returns | Attributable realized proceeds/distributions minus attributable acquisition/disposal costs and fees under an explicit lot/valuation policy | Anything derivable from aggregate payouts, wallet count, a snapshot or price change alone. Not supported in this plan |

Fee funding, accrual, claim and distribution can describe stages of the **same** economic transfer. Never add creator fees to their parent trading fees, or distributions to their funding, as independent “total yield.” Quote asset and reward asset are separate identities; matching tickers or a marketing name cannot equate them. NVDAX/GLDX/SPCXX/canonical STONK remain exact token identities, not evidence of underlying securities ownership or treasury entitlement.
